Mysore Pak

Besan aur bahut saare ghee ki melt-in-mouth mithai.

Time40 min
LevelHard
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianSouth IndianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Besan ko thode ghee mein halka bhun lo.

  2. 2

    Cheeni aur paani ki 1 taar ki chaashni banao. 8 min

  3. 3

    Chaashni mein besan daal kar milao.

  4. 4

    Garam ghee dheere dheere daalo aur lagataar hilao jab tak jaali na dikhe. 15 min

  5. 5

    Thali mein jama kar garam hi tukde kaat lo.

Desi Swap

Ghee ko garam hi daalna, tabhi mithai ke andar jaali banti hai.

Chef’s Notes

Mysore Pak Karnataka ki shahi mithai hai, jise Mysore ke raja ke rasoiye ne banaya tha.
