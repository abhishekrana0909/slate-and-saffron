Chipa

Kasava ke aate aur cheese ke chhote, karare bread-rings.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic50 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Paneer ko masal kar makkhan, doodh, saunf aur namak ke saath milao.

  2. 2

    Aata daal kar naram aata goondh lo.

  3. 3

    Chhote ring ya kangan jaisi shape banao. 8 min

  4. 4

    200°C par oven mein 20 minute phoolne aur sunehra hone tak bake karo. 20 min

  5. 5

    Garam garam chai ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Aata dheela lage toh aur thoda aata milao.

Chef’s Notes

Chipa Paraguay ka naashta hai jo har baazar aur bus stop par milta hai. Ise bina gluten ke tapioca ya kasava ke aate se banate hain.
