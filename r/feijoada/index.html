South America · Brazil

Vegetarian Feijoada

Gehre swaad wali Brazil ki black beans curry.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian version60 min · MediumSpice: Mild

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Beans raat bhar bhigo kar tej patte ke saath cooker mein naram karo. 30 min

  2. 2

    Pyaaz aur lehsun bhuno. Gajar, shimla mirch aur tamatar daalo. 8 min

  3. 3

    Paprika, jeera aur namak daalo.

  4. 4

    Beans daalo, kuch maslo aur gaadha hone tak pakao. 25 min

  5. 5

    Uble chawal aur chaho toh santre ki phaankon ke saath serve karo.

Desi Swap

Black beans na ho toh rajma lagbhag waisi hi curry dega.

Chef’s Notes

Brazil ki mashhoor dish paramparik roop se meat wali hai. Hamare mein beans aur dhuandaar swaad hi rehta hai.
