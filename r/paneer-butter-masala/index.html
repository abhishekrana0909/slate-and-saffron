Paneer Butter Masala

Mulayam paneer, tamatar aur makkhan ki reshmi gravy mein.

100% vegetarian35 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tamatar, pyaaz, kaju, adrak aur lehsun thode paani mein naram hone tak pakao. 12 min

  2. 2

    Thanda karke bareek peeso aur chaan lo.

  3. 3

    Makkhan pighlao. Mirch powder aur phir puree daalo. Gaadha hone tak pakao. 10 min

  4. 4

    Namak, cheeni, garam masala aur masli hui kasuri methi daalo.

  5. 5

    Paneer ke tukde aur cream daalo. Halki aanch pe paka kar serve karo. 4 min

Desi jugaad

Cream na ho toh 4 kaju doodh ke saath peeskar daal do.

Story of this dish

Ye malaidar tamatar gravy 1947 ke baad Delhi ke Punjabi restaurants se mashhoor hui.
