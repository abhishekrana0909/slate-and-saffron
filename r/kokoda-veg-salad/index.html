Kokoda, Vegetarian Version

Nariyal ke doodh aur nimbu mein tamatar, kheera aur paneer.

Time25 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianGluten-freeVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Paneer ko chhote tukdon mein kaat kar nimbu ke ras mein 10 minute bhigo do.

  2. 2

    Tamatar, kheera, pyaaz aur mirch kaat lo.

  3. 3

    Nariyal ka doodh aur namak milao.

  4. 4

    Saari cheezein mix karke fridge mein 15 minute rakho.

  5. 5

    Dhaniya chhidak kar thanda parso.

Desi Swap

Parosne se thodi der pehle hi nariyal ka doodh milao.

Chef’s Notes

Kokoda Fiji ki mashhoor dish hai jo aksar kachchi machhli se banti hai. Yahan paneer aur sabziyan hain.
