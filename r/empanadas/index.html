Cheese Corn Empanadas

Cheese aur corn se bhari haath se modi Argentina ki pastry.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version60 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maide aur namak mein makkhan maslo. Paani se goondh kar thanda karo. 30 min

  2. 2

    Pyaaz naram karo, corn aur paprika daal kar thanda karo. Cheese milao. 6 min

  3. 3

    Aata bel kar bade gol kaato. Bharo, modo aur kinare goontho.

  4. 4

    Doodh lagao. 200°C pe sunehra bake karo. 25 min

Desi jugaad

Oven na ho toh medium aanch pe tal lo.

Story of this dish

Spanish shabd empanar se, yaani bread mein lapetna. Meat aam hai, par cheese aur corn bhi classic hai.
