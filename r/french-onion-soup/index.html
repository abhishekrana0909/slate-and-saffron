French Onion Soup

Dheeme pakaye meethe pyaaz aur pighle cheese ka garam soup.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version75 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pyaaz ko patla kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Makkhan mein pyaaz ko dheemi aanch pe 35-40 minute gehra bhoora aur meetha hone tak bhuno. 40 min

  3. 3

    Thyme, tej patta aur stock daal kar 20 minute ubalo. 20 min

  4. 4

    Katori mein soup daalo, upar toast ki bread rakho aur cheese chhidko.

  5. 5

    Oven ya grill mein cheese ke pighal kar sunehra hone tak sek lo. 5 min

Desi jugaad

Pyaaz ko jaldi mat bhuno, dheemi aanch hi uska raaz hai.

Story of this dish

Ye soup aam taur par maans ke stock mein banta hai. Yahan sabzi ke stock se uska swaad bachaya gaya hai.
