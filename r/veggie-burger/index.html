Veggie Burger with Sweet Potato Fries

Rajma-chane ki tikki wala burger aur kurkure shakarkandi fries.

100% vegetarianVegan50 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Rajma, chane, oats, pyaaz, lehsun, paprika aur namak ko mota peeso aur tikkiyan banao.

  2. 2

    Tikkiyan tawe par dono taraf sunehri hone tak sekho. 10 min

  3. 3

    Shakarkandi ko lambe tukdon mein kaat kar tel aur namak lagao aur oven mein 20 minute sekho. 20 min

  4. 4

    Bun ko halka sek kar tamatar, kheera aur tikki rakho.

  5. 5

    Fries ke saath garam parso.

Desi Swap

Tikki ko 15 minute fridge mein rakhoge toh sekhte waqt tootegi nahi.

Chef’s Notes

Veggie burger ka vichaar aksar bhookhe vegetarian khane walon ke liye aaya. Aaj ye har burger shop ka hissa hai.
