Pasta Primavera

Taaza bahaar ki sabziyon ke saath halka pasta.

100% vegetarian30 min · Easy

Your rating

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pasta ko namak wale paani mein naram hone tak ubaalo aur thoda pasta ka paani bacha lo. 10 min

  2. 2

    Sabziyan patle tukdon mein kaat lo.

  3. 3

    Olive oil mein lehsun aur sabziyan tez aanch par 5 minute bhuno. 5 min

  4. 4

    Pasta, thoda pasta ka paani aur basil milao.

  5. 5

    Cheese aur kali mirch chhidak kar garam parso.

Desi jugaad

Sabziyan zyada na galaiye, thoda karara pan achha lagta hai.

Story of this dish

Primavera ka matlab hai bahaar. Ye pasta taaza sabziyon ke rang se bhara hota hai.
