Humitas

Taaze makke aur pyaaz ki naram, bhaap mein pakki potliyan.

100% vegetarianGluten-free70 min · Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Makke ke dane mixer mein mota peeso.

  2. 2

    Makkhan mein pyaaz naram karo, makka paste, paneer, cheeni aur namak milao aur 8 minute pakao. 8 min

  3. 3

    Har chhilke mein 2 chamach bharo aur lapet kar baandh do.

  4. 4

    Bhaap mein 40 minute pakao. 40 min

  5. 5

    Garam garam, salsa ya tamatar ki chutney ke saath parso.

Desi jugaad

Makke ki jagah taaza kaccha bhutta use karo, wahi asli swaad dega.

Story of this dish

Humitas Andes ke ilaake ka paramparik khaana hai. Taaza makka peeskar chhilke mein lapeta aur bhaap mein pakaya jata hai.
