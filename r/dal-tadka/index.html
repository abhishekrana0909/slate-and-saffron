Dal Tadka

Ghee ke garam tadke wali sukoon bhari peeli dal.

100% vegetarian30 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Dal dho lo. Haldi, namak aur 750 ml paani ke saath cooker mein naram hone tak pakao. 12 min

  2. 2

    Aadha ghee garam karo. Pyaaz bhuno, phir tamatar daal kar naram karo. 8 min

  3. 3

    Dal daal kar dheemi aanch pe pakao. 5 min

  4. 4

    Bacha ghee garam karo. Jeera, lehsun, hari mirch aur laal mirch daalo. Dal pe undel do.

  5. 5

    Upar hara dhaniya daal do.

Desi jugaad

Arhar na ho toh moong dal lo, jaldi pakti hai.

Story of this dish

Indian thali ki jaan. Aakhri tadka har masale ko jaga deta hai.
