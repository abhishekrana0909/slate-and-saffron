How to make it

  1. 1

    Chane raat bhar bhigo kar achhe se chaan lo.

  2. 2

    Pyaaz, lehsun aur herbs ke saath dardara peeso.

  3. 3

    Masale, namak aur baking powder mila kar fridge mein rakho. 30 min

  4. 4

    Chhoti tikki bana kar gehra sunehra talo. 5 min

  5. 5

    Tahini sauce ya pudina chutney ke saath serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Parsley na ho toh zyada hara dhaniya le lo.
