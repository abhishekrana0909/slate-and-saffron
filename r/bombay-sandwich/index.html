Bombay Veg Sandwich

Hari chutney, sabziyon aur masale wala grilled sandwich.

100% vegetarianStreet FoodMaharashtrian20 min · EasySpice: Spicy

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Dhaniya, pudina, hari mirch aur thode paani se hari chutney peeso.

  2. 2

    Ubale aloo aur chukandar, kheera, tamatar aur pyaaz ko patle gol tukdon mein kaat lo.

  3. 3

    Bread par makkhan aur hari chutney lagao.

  4. 4

    Sabziyon ki parat lagakar chaat masale jaisa mirch-namak chhidko aur doosri bread rakho. 3 min

  5. 5

    Grill ya tawe par makkhan ke saath sunehra hone tak sek kar tikone kaat do. 5 min

Desi Swap

Chaat masala chhidko aur ketchup ya chutney ke saath garam parso.

Chef’s Notes

Mumbai ki sadkon par thele par banne wala sandwich, jo hari chutney aur chaat masale se ban jata hai.
