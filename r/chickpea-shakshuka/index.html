Shakshuka without Egg, Chickpea Version

Chatpati tamatar-mirch ki sauce mein chane, bina ande ke.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeVegetarian version35 min · EasySpice: Mild

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Olive oil mein pyaaz aur shimla mirch naram karo, lehsun daalo.

  2. 2

    Tamatar, jeera, paprika aur namak daal kar 12 minute pakao jab tak sauce gaadhi ho jaye. 12 min

  3. 3

    Ubale chane daal kar 5 minute dheemi aanch pe pakao.

  4. 4

    Upar feta aur parsley chhidko.

  5. 5

    Kadhai se seedha roti ya bread ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Feta ki jagah paneer ke tukde bhi daal sakte ho.

Chef’s Notes

Shakshuka North Africa se Israel tak mashhoor hai aur ande ke saath banti hai. Yahan chane se wahi pet bharne wala swaad banaya gaya hai.
