Vegetable Paella

Kesar wale chawal aur taaza sabziyon ka kadhai bhar pakwan.

Time50 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chaudi kadhai mein olive oil garam karke shimla mirch aur beans bhuno.

  2. 2

    Lehsun, tamatar aur paprika daal kar masalte hue pakao. 6 min

  3. 3

    Chawal daal kar 2 minute chalao.

  4. 4

    Kesar wala garam stock daalo, ek baar hilao aur phir mat hilana. 18 minute dheemi aanch pe pakao. 18 min

  5. 5

    Matar daalo, 5 minute dhak do aur nimbu ke tukdon ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Neeche halka karara parat (socarrat) banna achhi paella ki pehchaan hai.

Chef’s Notes

Valencia ki asli paella mein seafood ya maans hota hai. Ye sabziyon wali shakahari roop hai.
