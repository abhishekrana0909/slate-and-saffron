Apple Crumble

Makkhan wale sunehre choore ke neeche garam masaledar seb.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic50 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Seb kaato. Aadhi cheeni, dalchini aur nimbu ka ras mila kar baking dish mein rakho.

  2. 2

    Thanda makkhan maide mein choora banne tak maslo.

  3. 3

    Bachi cheeni aur oats milao.

  4. 4

    Seb pe phailao. 180°C pe sunehra hone tak bake karo. 35 min

  5. 5

    Cream ke saath garam serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Oven na ho toh seb kadhai mein pakao aur choora alag bhuno.

Story of this dish

Britain ki pasand, jo doosre vishwa yuddh ke ration ke time se jodi jaati hai, jab pastry ka saamaan kam tha.
