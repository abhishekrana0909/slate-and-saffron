Jollof Rice

West Africa ka dhuandaar ek-bartan wala tamatar chawal.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version50 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tamatar, shimla mirch, ek pyaaz, adrak aur lehsun pees lo.

  2. 2

    Doosra pyaaz bhuno. Puree aur masale daal kar kuch minute bhuno. 4 min

  3. 3

    Pisa mixture daal kar gaadha hone tak pakao. 15 min

  4. 4

    Dhule chawal, stock, tej patta aur namak daalo. Kas kar dhak kar dheemi aanch pe pakao. 25 min

  5. 5

    Dhuandaar swaad ke liye neeche halka lagne do. Chala kar serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Stock na ho toh paani aur thoda zyada masala le lo.

Story of this dish

Senegambia ke Wolof logon ke naam par. Aksar meat ke stock mein banta hai, hamara sabzi ke stock mein.
