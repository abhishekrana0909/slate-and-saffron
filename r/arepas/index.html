Arepas with Black Beans and Cheese

Pighle cheese se bhari karari makke ki tikki.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic30 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Makke ka aata, namak aur gunguna paani mila kar rakho. 5 min

  2. 2

    Hatheli jitni moti tikki banao.

  3. 3

    Tel lage tawe pe dono taraf parat banne tak senko. 12 min

  4. 4

    Chaaku se khol kar cheese aur makkhan bharo.

  5. 5

    Black beans naram karke halka masal lo, namak daalo aur arepa mein cheese ke saath bharo.

Desi Swap

Normal makki ka aata mota hota hai. Garam paani se goondho aur halke haath se banao.

Chef’s Notes

Venezuela aur Colombia mein Europeans ke aane se pehle se khayi jaati makke ki tikki.
