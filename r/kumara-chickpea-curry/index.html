Kumara and Chickpea Curry

Shakarkandi aur chane ka nariyal-doodh wala halka curry.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free40 min · Easy

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tel mein pyaaz, adrak aur lehsun naram karo.

  2. 2

    Curry powder milao aur 1 minute bhuno.

  3. 3

    Shakarkandi ke tukde, ubale chane aur nariyal ka doodh daalo. 5 min

  4. 4

    Dheemi aanch par 20 minute pakao jab tak shakarkandi naram ho jaye. 20 min

  5. 5

    Dhaniya chhidak kar chawal ke saath parso.

Desi jugaad

Naram shakarkandi ka ek hissa masal do, curry gaadha ho jayega.

Story of this dish

Kumara New Zealand ki mashhoor shakarkandi hai, jo Maori khaane ka purana hissa hai.
