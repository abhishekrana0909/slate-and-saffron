Africa / Egypt

Koshari

Chawal, dal aur pasta, upar teekha tamatar aur karara pyaaz.

100% vegetarian50 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chane raat bhar bhigo kar naram ubaalo, ya canned wale le lo. 20 min

  2. 2

    Patla kata pyaaz karara talo. Tel bacha kar rakho. 12 min

  3. 3

    Pyaaz wale tel aur paani mein dal-chawal saath pakao. 20 min

  4. 4

    Macaroni alag ubaalo. 9 min

  5. 5

    Puree, lehsun, jeera, mirch, sirka aur namak pakao. 10 min

  6. 6

    Sab parat mein lagao, upar sauce aur karara pyaaz daalo.

Desi jugaad

Laal masoor aasani se milti hai. Bhoori dal zyada paramparik hai.

Story of this dish

Egypt ka sabse pasandida street food, jo parampara se hi shakahari hai.
