Potato Gratin Dauphinois

Patle aloo ke tukde, malai aur lehsun mein sunehre sike hue.

100% vegetarianGluten-free80 min · Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo chheel kar bahut patle gol tukde kaat lo aur dho kar na dhona (starch bacha rehta hai).

  2. 2

    Doodh, cream, lehsun, jaiphal, namak aur kali mirch ek pateele mein 5 minute garam karo. 5 min

  3. 3

    Baking dish par makkhan lagao aur aloo ki parat dar parat lagakar upar cream daalo.

  4. 4

    170°C par oven mein 1 ghante tak bake karo jab tak upar sunehra aur aloo naram na ho jayein. 60 min

Desi jugaad

Oven mein dhakkan se dhak do aur aakhri 15 minute kholo.

Story of this dish

France ke Dauphiné ilaake ka mashhoor pakwan, jo dheemi aanch pe ghante bhar pakta hai.
