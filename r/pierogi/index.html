Potato Cheese Pierogi

Aloo aur paneer se bhari Poland ki aadhe chaand jaisi dumplings.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version70 min · Hard

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, namak, gunguna paani aur ek chammach makkhan se naram aata goondh kar rakho. 30 min

  2. 2

    Aloo ubaal kar maslo. Masla paneer, kali mirch aur namak milao. 15 min

  3. 3

    Aata patla belo, gol kaato, bharo, mod kar band karo.

  4. 4

    Namak wale paani mein upar tairne tak ubaalo, phir ek minute aur. 4 min

  5. 5

    Pyaaz makkhan mein sunehra karo. Upar daal kar serve karo. 8 min

Desi jugaad

Asli recipe mein twarog cheese hota hai. India mein paneer sabse kareebi option hai.

Story of this dish

Pierogi ruskie Poland ka pasandida khaana hai. Kai aaton mein anda hota hai, hamare mein nahi.
