Lychee Rose Cooler

Lychee, gulab aur nimbu ka thanda meetha drink.

Time8 min
LevelEasy
Alcohol0%
Serves4

0% alcoholVeganGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Jug mein lychee ko chamche se halka kuchal lo.

  2. 2

    Rose syrup, nimbu ras aur pudina daalo.

  3. 3

    Soda aur khoob saari barf daalo.

  4. 4

    Halke haath se mila kar thanda serve karo.

Desi Swap

Tin wale lychee ke syrup se thoda rose syrup kam kar sakte ho.

Chef’s Notes

Lychee aur gulab ki jodi China mein garmiyon ki chai ki dukaanon pe milti hai.
