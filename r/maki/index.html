Avocado Cucumber Maki

Taazi sabziyon wale saaf-suthre Japanese chawal rolls.

100% vegetarianVeganNo onion, no garlic45 min · HardSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chawal achhe se dho kar normal se thode kam paani mein pakao. 15 min

  2. 2

    Sirka, cheeni aur namak milao. Pankha karke thanda karo.

  3. 3

    Sabziyon ki lambi patli pattiyan kaato.

  4. 4

    Nori pe chawal phailao, upar kinara chhodo, sabziyan rakh kar kas kar roll karo.

  5. 5

    Geele chaaku se kaato. Soy sauce ke saath serve karo.

Desi Swap

Sushi mat na ho toh saaf kapde pe cling film laga lo.

Avocado na ho toh shimla mirch ya tale paneer ki pattiyan le lo.

Chef’s Notes

Maki ka matlab hai lapeta hua. Kheere wale rolls Japanese sushi ka paramparik hissa hain.
