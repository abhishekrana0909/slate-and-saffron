Brazilian Lemonade

Condensed milk wala malaidar poore nimbu ka drink.

0% alcoholNo onion, no garlic5 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Nimbu achhe se dho kar chaar tukde karo.

  2. 2

    Paani aur cheeni ke saath bas kuch second peeso.

  3. 3

    Achhe se chaan lo, phir condensed milk aur barf ke saath dobara peeso.

  4. 4

    Turant serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Nimbu thodi der hi peeso. Zyada peesne se kadwa ho jata hai.

Story of this dish

Brazil mein ise limonada suica kehte hain, aur ye poore phal ke saath peesa jata hai.
