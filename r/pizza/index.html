Pizza Margherita

Karare base pe tamatar, mozzarella aur basil.

100% vegetarian40 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Gungune paani mein yeast aur cheeni milao. Maida, namak aur tel daal kar chikna goondho aur phoolne do. 60 min

  2. 2

    Tamatar puree, lehsun aur namak kuch minute pakao. 5 min

  3. 3

    Oven ko sabse oonche temperature pe tray ke saath garam karo. 15 min

  4. 4

    Aate ko patla phailao. Sauce aur todi hui mozzarella daalo.

  5. 5

    Phaphole padne tak bake karo. Basil aur olive oil daalo. 9 min

Desi jugaad

Oven na ho toh bhaari kadhai mein dhak kar dheemi aanch pe pakao.

Story of this dish

Lok katha ise 1889 ke Naples aur Queen Margherita se jodti hai, jisme Italy ke jhande ke rang hain.
