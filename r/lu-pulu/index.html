Lu Pulu, Vegetarian Version

Taro ke patton mein nariyal ke doodh ke saath pakka hua palak-pyaaz.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian version70 min · MediumSpice: Mild

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Palak ko achhe se dho kar mota kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Pyaaz ko kaat kar palak aur namak ke saath milao.

  3. 3

    Kele ke patte par bharwan rakh kar upar nariyal ka doodh daalo aur parcel banao.

  4. 4

    Foil mein lapeto aur 180°C par 40 minute bake karo. 40 min

  5. 5

    Garam parso.

Desi Swap

Taro ke patte na milen toh palak hi sabse achha vikalp hai.

Chef’s Notes

Lu pulu Tonga ka mashhoor khaana hai jo aksar corned beef ke saath banta hai. Yahan sirf palak, pyaaz aur nariyal ka doodh hai.
