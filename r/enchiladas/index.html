Vegetarian Enchiladas

Rajma aur sabziyon se bhare tortilla, lal sauce mein.

Time50 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianGluten-free

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pyaaz, shimla mirch aur corn ko tel mein bhuno, black beans milao.

  2. 2

    Tamatar puree, mirch, jeera aur namak se lal sauce pakao. 10 min

  3. 3

    Tortilla ko sauce mein dubo kar bharwan bharo aur rolls banao.

  4. 4

    Dish mein rolls rakho, upar sauce aur cheese daalo.

  5. 5

    190°C par oven mein 20 minute bake karo. 20 min

Desi Swap

Oven na ho toh kadhai mein dhak kar dheemi aanch par pighla lo.

Chef’s Notes

Enchilada ka matlab hai mirch mein lapeta hua. Tortilla ko lal mirch ki sauce mein dubo kar bake kiya jata hai.
