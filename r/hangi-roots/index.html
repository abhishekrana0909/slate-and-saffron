Hangi-style Roots

Zameen ke andar pakne jaise, dhuyen ki khushbu wali bhuni sabziyan.

Time80 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianGluten-freeVegetarian versionNo onion, no garlic

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo, shakarkandi, kaddu aur gajar ke bade tukde kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Tel, makkhan aur namak lagao.

  3. 3

    Foil mein lapeto aur upar patta gobhi ke patte rakho.

  4. 4

    200°C par oven mein 50 minute pakao. 50 min

  5. 5

    Garam garam makkhan ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Dhuyen ki khushbu ke liye foil kholne se pehle thoda sa smoked paprika daalo.

Chef’s Notes

Hangi Maori ka paramparik tareeka hai jisme garam patthar par khaana zameen mein pakaya jata hai. Ghar mein ye oven mein hangi-style banta hai.
