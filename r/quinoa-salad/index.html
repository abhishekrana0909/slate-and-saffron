Andean Quinoa Salad

Karari sabziyon wala nimbu ka taaza quinoa.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic25 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Quinoa achhe se dho lo. Dugne paani mein dhak kar pakao. 15 min

  2. 2

    Phaila kar thanda hone do. 10 min

  3. 3

    Saari sabziyan chhoti kaato.

  4. 4

    Nimbu ras, tel, jeera aur namak milao.

  5. 5

    Dhaniye ke saath sab mila do.

Desi jugaad

Quinoa na ho toh paka daliya ya bajra le lo.

Story of this dish

Andes mein hazaaron saal se ugta hai. UN ne 2013 ko International Year of Quinoa ghoshit kiya tha.
