Spaghetti al Pomodoro

Saadi aur taazi tamatar sauce mein spaghetti.

100% vegetarian25 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tamatar halka ubaalo, chheelo aur masal lo. 3 min

  2. 2

    Tel mein kata lehsun dheemi aanch pe khushboo aane tak garam karo. 2 min

  3. 3

    Tamatar, namak aur cheeni daalo. Gaadha hone tak pakao. 15 min

  4. 4

    Namak wale paani mein spaghetti bas naram hone tak ubaalo. 10 min

  5. 5

    Thode pasta paani aur basil ke saath sauce mein milao.

Desi jugaad

Fresh basil na ho toh sukha oregano le lo.

Story of this dish

Tamatar America se Italy pahunche. Ye saadi sauce batati hai ki achhe pasta ko kitni kam cheezein chahiye.
