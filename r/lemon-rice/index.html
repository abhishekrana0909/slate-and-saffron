Lemon Rice

Haldi aur nimbu ke tadke wale khatte chawal.

100% vegetarianSouth IndianVeganGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic25 min · Easy

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chawal pakao aur thanda karke dane alag kar lo.

  2. 2

    Tel mein rai, chana dal aur moongfali tadkao, phir curry patta, hari mirch aur haldi daalo. 4 min

  3. 3

    Tadka chawal mein daal kar namak ke saath halke haath se milao.

  4. 4

    Gas band karke nimbu ka ras daalo aur hara dhaniya milao.

Desi jugaad

Nimbu aakhir mein daalo, garam mein daalne se kadwa ho jata hai.

Story of this dish

Dakshin Bharat ka tiffin aur mandir ke prasad ka pasandida chawal, jo bacha hua chawal bhi naya bana deta hai.
