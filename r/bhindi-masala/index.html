Bhindi Masala

Karari bhindi, pyaaz-masale ke saath, bina chipchipe.

100% vegetarianNorth IndianVeganGluten-free30 min · Easy

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Bhindi dho kar poori tarah poncho aur 1 inch ke tukde kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Tel mein bhindi tez aanch pe 5 minute bhuno aur nikal lo. 5 min

  3. 3

    Usi tel mein jeera aur pyaaz sunehra bhuno, phir tamatar aur masale daalo. 8 min

  4. 4

    Bhindi wapas daal kar 5 minute halke se milao. Amchur daal kar parso. 5 min

Desi jugaad

Bhindi mein namak aakhir mein daalo, warna wo paani chhodti hai.

Story of this dish

Bhindi ki sabzi tabhi acchi banti hai jab wo chipchipi na ho, aur uska raaz hai usse sukha kar kaatna.
