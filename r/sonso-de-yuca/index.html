Sonso de Yuca

Kasava aur cheese ko bhoona hua lamba kebab-numa naashta.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic50 min · Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Kasava ko ubaal kar naram karo. 20 min

  2. 2

    Garam hi masal kar paneer, makkhan aur namak milao.

  3. 3

    Lambi belan jaisi shape do aur seekh ya lakdi ke tukde par lapeto.

  4. 4

    Garam tawe ya aanch par dono taraf 12 minute sunehra sekho. 12 min

  5. 5

    Garam garam parso.

Desi jugaad

Kasava na mile toh shakarkandi aur aloo ka mix chalega.

Story of this dish

Sonso Colombia ke Caribbean tat ka khaana hai. Isme kasava (yuca) aur cheese ko milakar sekha jata hai.
