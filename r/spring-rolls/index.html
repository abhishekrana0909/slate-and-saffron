Vegetable Spring Rolls

Karari sabziyon se bhare sunehre rolls.

100% vegetarian45 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, namak aur paani ka naram aata goondho. Thodi der baad bahut patli square sheets bel kar tawe pe halka senko. 20 min

  2. 2

    Lehsun aur sabziyan tez aanch pe bhuno. Soy sauce, kali mirch, namak daal kar thanda karo. 4 min

  3. 3

    Sheet pe masala rakho, kinare mod kar kas kar roll karo. Maide ke ghol se chipkao.

  4. 4

    Medium aanch pe sunehra talo. 5 min

Desi jugaad

Time kam ho toh ready spring roll sheets le lo.

Story of this dish

China ke Spring Festival se juda naam, jab ye sunehre rolls khushhaali ki dua ke saath serve hote hain.
