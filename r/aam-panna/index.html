Aam Panna

Bhune kachche aam ka khatta-meetha garmi wala drink.

0% alcoholNo onion, no garlic25 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Kachche aam naram hone tak ubaalo, phir thanda karo. 15 min

  2. 2

    Gooda nichod lo, chhilka aur guthli hata do.

  3. 3

    Gooda, cheeni, jeera, kala namak aur pudina thode paani ke saath peeso.

  4. 4

    Baaki paani milao aur barf ke saath serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Aam ko aanch pe bhunoge toh dhuandaar swaad aayega.

Story of this dish

North India ki garmiyon ki pasand, jo tab banta hai jab aam abhi kachche hote hain.
