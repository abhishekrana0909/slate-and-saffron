Masala Dosa

Karara sunehra dosa aur masaledar naram aloo.

100% vegetarianSouth IndianVeganGluten-free40 min · HardSpice: Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chawal aur urad alag alag 5 ghante bhigo do. Bareek peeso, milao aur raat bhar khameer uthne do.

  2. 2

    Aloo ubaal kar mota masal lo. 15 min

  3. 3

    Tel garam karo, rai aur curry patta tadkao. Pyaaz aur mirch naram karo, haldi, aloo aur namak daalo. 6 min

  4. 4

    Garam tawe pe ek kalchi ghol patla phailao. Tel daal kar karara hone tak senko. 2 min

  5. 5

    Aloo ka masala rakho, modo aur chutney ke saath serve karo.

Desi Swap

Time kam ho toh bazaar ka ready dosa batter le lo.

Chef’s Notes

South India ka classic. Chawal aur dal ke khameer wale ghol se iska halka khatta swaad aata hai.
