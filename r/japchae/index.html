Japchae

Rangeen sabziyon wale chamakdar Korean glass noodles.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version30 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Noodles naram hone tak ubaalo, dho lo aur kainchi se kaat lo. 6 min

  2. 2

    Soy sauce, cheeni, til ka tel aur lehsun milao.

  3. 3

    Palak halka ubaal kar nichod lo. 1 min

  4. 4

    Pyaaz, gajar, shimla mirch aur mushroom baari baari bhuno. 6 min

  5. 5

    Noodles, sabzi aur sauce milao. Upar til chhidko. 3 min

Desi jugaad

Glass noodles na ho toh patli rice sevaiyan le lo.

Story of this dish

Korea ki tyohaar wali dish, jo aksar meat ke saath banti hai. Hamari sirf sabziyon wali hai.
