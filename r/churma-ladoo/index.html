Churma Ladoo

Baati ke churme se bane ghee aur gud ke ladoo.

Time40 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianRajasthaniNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Atta mein ghee ka moyan daal kar sakht goondho aur moti baatiyan banao.

  2. 2

    Baatiyon ko dheemi aanch par bhura hone tak sek lo. 20 min

  3. 3

    Thanda kar mixer mein dar-dara peeso.

  4. 4

    Garam ghee, gud aur elaichi milao.

  5. 5

    Haath se gol ladoo bana kar badam se sajao.

Desi Swap

Ghee garam hi milana, tabhi ladoo bandhte hain.

Chef’s Notes

Baati ko kootkar churma banta hai, aur uske ladoo prasad mein bhi chadhaye jate hain.
