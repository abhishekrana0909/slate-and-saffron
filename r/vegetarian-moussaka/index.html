Vegetarian Moussaka

Baingan, aloo aur masoor ki dal ke parat wali bake.

Time90 min
LevelHard
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Baingan aur aloo ke gol tukde olive oil mein sunehre sek lo.

  2. 2

    Masoor dal ko pyaaz, lehsun, tamatar puree, dalchini aur thyme ke saath 20 minute naram pakao. 20 min

  3. 3

    Makkhan mein maida bhuno, doodh daal kar gaadhi safed sauce (bechamel) banao, cheese milao. 10 min

  4. 4

    Dish mein aloo, dal ka masala, baingan ki parat lagao aur upar sauce daalo.

  5. 5

    180°C par oven mein 35 minute sunehra hone tak bake karo. 35 min

Desi Swap

Parosne se pehle 10 minute rakh do, tabhi tukde sahi kaat paoge.

Chef’s Notes

Moussaka mein aam taur par maans hota hai. Yahan masoor dal se uska bharpoor swaad banaya gaya hai.
