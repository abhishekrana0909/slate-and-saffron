Greek Spanakopita

Palak aur feta ki karari filo parat wali pie.

100% vegetarian70 min · HardSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Palak aur pyaaz ko halka bhuno aur nichod kar paani nikaal do.

  2. 2

    Isme crumbled feta, dill, kali mirch aur namak milao.

  3. 3

    Baking dish mein filo ki sheet par pighla makkhan lagao, 5 sheet ek ke upar ek rakho. 10 min

  4. 4

    Bharwan phailao aur baaki sheets upar se makkhan lagakar daalo.

  5. 5

    180°C par 35 minute sunehra aur karara hone tak bake karo. 35 min

Desi Swap

Filo ko geele kapde se dhak ke rakho, wo jaldi sookh kar toot jati hai.

Chef’s Notes

Greece ki bakery ka sabse mashhoor pie, jo naashte aur chai ke waqt dono mein parosa jata hai.
