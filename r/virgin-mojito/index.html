Virgin Mojito

Masla pudina, nimbu aur soda, khoob saari barf pe.

0% alcoholNo onion, no garlic5 min · Easy

This drink usually has alcohol. Ours does not.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Har glass mein pudina, nimbu ke tukde aur cheeni saath dabao.

  2. 2

    Glass ko kuchli barf se bhar do.

  3. 3

    Upar soda daal kar neeche se milao.

Desi jugaad

Pudina halke se dabao. Zyada phadne se drink kadwa ho jata hai.

Story of this dish

Mojito Cuba se aaya hai. Hamare mein pudina aur nimbu hai, alcohol nahi.
