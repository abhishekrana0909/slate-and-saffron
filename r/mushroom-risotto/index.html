Mushroom Risotto

Malaidar chawal aur bhune mushroom, dheeme dheeme paka hua.

100% vegetarianGluten-free45 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Mushroom ke tukde makkhan mein sunehre hone tak bhuno aur alag rakh do. 8 min

  2. 2

    Usi makkhan mein pyaaz aur lehsun naram karo.

  3. 3

    Arborio chawal daal kar 2 minute chalao taaki wo halke chamak jayein. 2 min

  4. 4

    Garam stock ek ek karchhi daalte hue chawal ko lagataar hilao, jab tak wo sooch kar ghul na jaye. 18 min

  5. 5

    Bhune mushroom, cheese, makkhan aur kali mirch milao.

  6. 6

    Parsley daal kar turant garam parso.

Desi Swap

Parmesan asli mein rennet se banta hai, isliye 'vegetarian' likha hard cheese dhundho.

Chef’s Notes

Italy ke Lombardy ka khaana, jahan chawal ko dheere dheere stock daal kar malai jaisa banaya jata hai.
