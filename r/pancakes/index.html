Eggless Maple Pancakes

Sunehre maple syrup mein doobe phoole pancakes.

100% vegetarianVegetarian versionNo onion, no garlic20 min · Easy

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, cheeni, baking powder aur namak milao.

  2. 2

    Doodh, dahi, pighla makkhan aur vanilla daalo. Bas milne tak milao.

  3. 3

    Ghol ko rakho. 5 min

  4. 4

    Makkhan lage tawe pe chhote gol daalo. Bulbule aane pe palto. 3 min

  5. 5

    Ek ke upar ek rakh kar maple syrup daalo.

Desi jugaad

Maple syrup na ho toh shahad ya gud ki chashni le lo.

Story of this dish

Duniya ka zyadatar maple syrup Canada mein banta hai. Pancakes mein aksar anda hota hai, inme nahi.
