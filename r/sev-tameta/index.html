Sev Tameta nu Shaak

Tamatar ki khatti-meethi sabzi jisme sev dal kar parosi jaati hai.

LevelEasy
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianGujaratiVeganGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tel mein rai ka tadka lagao.

  2. 2

    Kate tamatar, haldi, mirch aur cheeni daalo.

  3. 3

    Dheemi aanch par naram hone tak pakao. 10 min

  4. 4

    Parosne se pehle sev daal kar dhaniya chhidko.
