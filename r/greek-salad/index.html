Greek Salad

Tamatar, kheera, olives aur feta ka tukda.

100% vegetarian10 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tamatar aur kheere ke bade tukde kaato.

  2. 2

    Kata pyaaz, shimla mirch aur olives daalo.

  3. 3

    Namak aur olive oil daal kar halke se milao.

  4. 4

    Upar feta rakh kar oregano chhidko.

Desi jugaad

Feta na ho toh namak laga paneer le lo.

Story of this dish

Greece mein ise horiatiki yaani gaon ka salad kehte hain. Isme lettuce nahi hota.
