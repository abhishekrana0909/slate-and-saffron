Vegetarian Chili

Poori tarah rajma-beans se bani dhuandaar Texas style chili.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeVegetarian version40 min · Easy

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Beans raat bhar bhigo kar cooker mein naram karo. 25 min

  2. 2

    Pyaaz, shimla mirch aur lehsun bhuno. 6 min

  3. 3

    Masale, phir tamatar daal kar galao. 8 min

  4. 4

    Beans, corn, namak aur thoda paani daal kar gaadha hone tak pakao. 20 min

  5. 5

    Upar dahi daal kar serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Black beans na ho toh rajma badha do.

Story of this dish

Chili Texas ki official dish hai, jo aam taur pe meat se banti hai. Hamari poori beans se.
