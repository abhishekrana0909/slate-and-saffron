Africa / Ethiopia

Misir Wat

Gehre laal rang ki teekhi Ethiopian dal.

100% vegetarian45 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pyaaz bahut bareek kaato. Tel mein dheemi aanch pe naram aur meetha hone tak pakao. 15 min

  2. 2

    Lehsun, adrak aur berbere daal kar do minute chalao. 2 min

  3. 3

    Kata tamatar daal kar galao. 5 min

  4. 4

    Dhuli dal aur paani daalo. Bahut naram hone tak pakao. 25 min

  5. 5

    Swaad anusaar namak daal kar bread ya chawal ke saath serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Berbere na ho toh laal mirch, paprika, sonth, elaichi, dalchini aur laung mila lo.

Injera na ho toh dosa achha saathi hai.

Story of this dish

Ethiopia mein vrat ke dinon ka main khaana, jab bahut log animal food nahi lete. Paramparik roop se injera ke saath khaya jaata hai.
