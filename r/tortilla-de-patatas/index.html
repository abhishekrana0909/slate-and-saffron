Spanish Tortilla de Patatas

Aloo-pyaaz ka mote parat jaisa spanish tortilla, bina ande ka.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian version50 min · MediumSpice: Mild

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo aur pyaaz patle kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Olive oil mein aloo aur pyaaz dheemi aanch pe naram hone tak 20 minute pakao. 20 min

  3. 3

    Besan, 1 gilaas paani, haldi aur namak ko gaanth na rahe tak phento aur isme pakey aloo milao. 5 min

  4. 4

    Kadhai mein thoda tel garam karke mixture daalo aur dhak kar dheemi aanch pe 8 minute pakao. 8 min

  5. 5

    Plate ki madad se ulta kar ke doosri taraf bhi 5 minute pakao aur wedge mein kaat kar parso. 5 min

Desi Swap

Besan ka ghol chhoti pooriyon jaisa gaadha hona chahiye, patla nahi.

Chef’s Notes

Asli tortilla ande se banti hai. Yahan besan ke ghol se wahi naram kaatne layak parat banaya gaya hai.
