Causa Rellena, Vegetarian Version

Peele aloo ki thandi parat mein avocado aur sabzi ki filling.

Time50 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian versionNo onion, no garlic

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo ubaal kar garam garam masal lo. 20 min

  2. 2

    Nimbu, tel, mirch ka paste aur namak mila kar naram aata banao.

  3. 3

    Avocado ko masal kar nimbu aur namak milao.

  4. 4

    Dish mein aloo ki parat, phir avocado aur tamatar, phir dobara aloo ki parat lagao.

  5. 5

    Fridge mein 30 minute thanda karke katorne kaat kar parso. 30 min

Desi Swap

Aloo garam hi masalna, thande mein gaanth rahegi.

Chef’s Notes

Causa Peru ka thanda aloo ka terrine hai, jisme aksar tuna ya murgh bharta hai. Yahan avocado aur sabziyan hain.
