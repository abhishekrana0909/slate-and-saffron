Injera with Vegetarian Vegetable Platter

Khatti injera par rangeen dal-sabzi ki bhari thali.

100% vegetarianVegan60 min · Hard

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Teff ya ragi ke aate ko paani mein ghol kar 2 din dhak kar khameer uthne do.

  2. 2

    Patle ghol ko non-stick tawe par dhak kar 2 minute pakao, palto nahi. 20 min

  3. 3

    Misir wat jaisi lal masoor, alag se patta gobhi-gajar aur palak ki sabzi pakao. 25 min

  4. 4

    Injera ko plate par phailao aur beech mein sabziyan alag alag ginti mein rakho.

  5. 5

    Chhoti injera ko haath se tod kar sabzi uthate hue khao.

Desi jugaad

Teff na mile toh ragi ka aata ya dosa ghol se milti-julti roti banao.

Story of this dish

Injera ek khamiri roti hai jiske upar saari sabziyan saath mein parosi jati hain aur haath se khayi jati hain.
