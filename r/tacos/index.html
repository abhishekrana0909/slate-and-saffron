Vegetarian Tacos

Masaledar chane aur nimbu ke saath naram tortilla.

Time25 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Bhige chane naram ubaalo ya canned wale le lo. 20 min

  2. 2

    Pyaaz aur shimla mirch bhuno. Chane, jeera, paprika aur namak daalo. 6 min

  3. 3

    Tamatar ko dhaniya aur nimbu ras ke saath kaato.

  4. 4

    Tortilla tawe pe garam karo. 1 min

  5. 5

    Chane, salsa aur avocado bharo.

Desi Swap

Tortilla na ho toh patli makki ya gehun ki roti le lo.

Chef’s Notes

Mexico ka haath mein khane wala classic, aksar meat se bhara. Hamara chane se bana hai.
