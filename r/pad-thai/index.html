Vegetable Pad Thai

Thailand ke khatte-meethe, moongfali wale rice noodles.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version25 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Noodles garam paani mein bas lachile hone tak bhigo kar chaan lo. 8 min

  2. 2

    Imli, soy sauce, cheeni aur mirch mila kar sauce banao.

  3. 3

    Tofu sunehra talo. Lehsun aur gajar daalo. 5 min

  4. 4

    Noodles aur sauce daal kar tez aanch pe milao. 3 min

  5. 5

    Ankurit moong aur hara pyaaz daalo. Upar moongfali aur nimbu daalo.

Desi jugaad

Tofu na ho toh halka tala paneer le lo.

Rice noodles na ho toh patle chapte noodles le lo.

Story of this dish

Thailand ki sarkaar ne 1930-40 ke dashak mein pad thai ko national dish ki tarah badhava diya. Humne fish sauce aur anda hata diya hai.
