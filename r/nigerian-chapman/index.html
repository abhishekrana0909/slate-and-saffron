Chapman

Santra, anaar aur nimbu ka Nigeria ka party mocktail.

Time10 min
LevelEasy
Alcohol0%
Serves4

0% alcoholVeganGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

How to make it

  1. 1

    Santra aur nimbu nichod kar anaar ka juice jug mein daalo.

  2. 2

    Kheera patla kaat kar daalo.

  3. 3

    Barf bharo aur soda daalo.

  4. 4

    Mila kar nimbu ki slice ke saath serve karo.

Desi Swap

Grenadine ki kuch boondein rang aur gehra kar deti hain.

Chef’s Notes

Chapman Nigeria ki party ka favourite hai, jo hamesha bina alcohol ke hi banta hai.
