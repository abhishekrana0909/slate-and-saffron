Vegetable Couscous

Halki masaledar sabzi ke saath phoola hua couscous.

100% vegetarian35 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tel mein pyaaz naram karo. Puree aur masale daalo. 5 min

  2. 2

    Gajar, chane, paani aur namak daal kar pakao. 15 min

  3. 3

    Zucchini daal kar naram karo. 6 min

  4. 4

    Couscous pe ubalta namkeen paani daalo, dhak kar rakho. Kaante se kholo. 5 min

  5. 5

    Couscous ke upar sabzi aur shorba daal kar serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Couscous na ho toh bhuni sooji se kaam chal jayega.

Story of this dish

Maghreb ki couscous parampara ko 2020 mein UNESCO ki virasat list mein shamil kiya gaya.
