Gulab Jamun

Gulab ki chashni mein doobe naram khoye ke gole.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic45 min · Hard

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Cheeni, paani aur elaichi 5 minute ubaalo. Gulab jal daal kar garam rakho. 5 min

  2. 2

    Khoya, maida aur soda halke haath se goondho, chikna hone tak doodh milao.

  3. 3

    Bina daraar wali chhoti goliyan banao.

  4. 4

    Dheemi aanch pe ghee mein gehra bhoora hone tak halke halke talo. 10 min

  5. 5

    Serve karne se pehle garam chashni mein bhigo do. 60 min

Desi jugaad

Khoya na ho toh milk powder mein thodi cream aur ghee mila lo.

Story of this dish

Iska naam gulab jal aur jamun phal se milkar bana hai, jaisa ye dikhta hai.
