Halloumi Burger

Seka halloumi, tamatar aur beetroot ka Aussie-style burger.

Time25 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Halloumi ko moti slice mein kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Tawe par tel lagakar dono taraf sunehra sek lo. 6 min

  3. 3

    Bun ko halka sek lo.

  4. 4

    Bun par mayo, patta, tamatar, beetroot aur halloumi lagao.

  5. 5

    Garam parso.

Desi Swap

Halloumi ko sekhte waqt chhedo mat, tab wo sunehra banta hai.

Chef’s Notes

Australian burger ki khaas pehchaan beetroot ki slice hai, jise yahan halloumi ke saath jodakar parosa gaya hai.
