Amritsari Kulcha

Aloo-paneer bhara tandoori jaisa kulcha, chole ke saath.

Time60 min
LevelHard
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianNorth IndianStreet Food

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, dahi, baking powder aur namak se naram aata goondh kar dhak do. 30 min

  2. 2

    Ubale aloo, paneer, pyaaz, ajwain, amchur aur namak milakar bharwan banao.

  3. 3

    Aate ki loi mein bharwan bhar kar halke haath se gol bel lo.

  4. 4

    Garam tawe pe dono taraf sunehra hone tak senko. 6 min

  5. 5

    Upar makkhan lagao aur chole ke saath garam parso.

Desi Swap

Tandoor na ho toh dhakkar rakhe tawe par sekne se bhi naram kulcha banta hai.

Chef’s Notes

Amritsar ki galiyon ka mashhoor naashta, jo makkhan aur chole ke saath parosa jata hai.
