Damper Bread

Sunehri parat wali Australia ki saadi bread.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic45 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Oven 200°C pe garam karo. Maida, baking powder aur namak milao. 10 min

  2. 2

    Makkhan maslo. Doodh daal kar naram aata banao.

  3. 3

    Gol bana kar upar cross ka cut lagao.

  4. 4

    Thapthapane pe khokhli aawaz aane tak bake karo. 30 min

Desi jugaad

Oven na ho toh bhaari dhake bartan mein dheemi aanch pe banao.

Story of this dish
