Australian Damper with Herb Butter

Sunehri parat wali Australia ki saadi bread.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic45 min · EasySpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Oven 200°C pe garam karo. Maida, baking powder aur namak milao. 10 min

  2. 2

    Makkhan maslo. Doodh daal kar naram aata banao.

  3. 3

    Gol bana kar upar cross ka cut lagao.

  4. 4

    Thapthapane pe khokhli aawaz aane tak bake karo. 30 min

  5. 5

    Makkhan mein kata parsley aur hara dhaniya milao aur garam damper par lagao.

Desi Swap

Oven na ho toh bhaari dhake bartan mein dheemi aanch pe banao.

Chef’s Notes

Australia ke charwahe aur yatri ise campfire ke angaaron mein banate the.
