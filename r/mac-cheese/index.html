How to make it

  1. 1

    Namak wale paani mein macaroni ubaalo. 9 min

  2. 2

    Makkhan pighla kar maida daalo aur thoda bhuno. 2 min

  3. 3

    Dheere dheere doodh milate hue gaadha hone tak pakao. 6 min

  4. 4

    Aanch band karke cheese, mustard, kali mirch aur namak daalo.

  5. 5

    Macaroni mila kar garam serve karo.
