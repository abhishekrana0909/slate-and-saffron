Roast Pumpkin and Feta Salad

Bhuna kaddu, feta aur hare patton ki halki salad.

Time40 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Kaddu ke tukdon par olive oil, namak aur kali mirch lagao.

  2. 2

    200°C par oven mein 25 minute sunehra hone tak sekho. 25 min

  3. 3

    Patton ko plate mein sajao.

  4. 4

    Upar garam kaddu, feta ka chura aur beej daalo.

  5. 5

    Nimbu ka ras aur olive oil chhidak kar parso.

Desi Swap

Oven na ho toh kadhai mein dhak kar sekho.

Chef’s Notes

Australia ka kaddu bahut mashhoor hai. Bhune kaddu ki meethaas feta ke namkeen swaad se achhi jodi banati hai.
