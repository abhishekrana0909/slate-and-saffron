Eggplant Parmigiana

Talle baingan, tamatar sauce aur pighle cheese ki parat.

100% vegetarian70 min · Hard

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Baingan ke patle gol tukde kaat kar namak lagakar 15 minute rakh do aur paani poncho.

  2. 2

    Tukdon par maida, phir thode paani mein dubo kar breadcrumbs lagao aur olive oil mein sunehra sekh lo. 15 min

  3. 3

    Lehsun aur tamatar puree se 10 minute sauce pakao, basil daalo. 10 min

  4. 4

    Dish mein sauce, baingan, mozzarella aur parmesan ki parat lagao aur dohrao.

  5. 5

    200°C par oven mein 25 minute bake karo jab tak upar sunehra na ho jaye. 25 min

Desi jugaad

Oven na ho toh kadhai mein dhak kar dheemi aanch pe pakao.

Story of this dish

Southern Italy ka tyohaar wala khaana, jise parat dar parat banakar oven mein sekte hain.
