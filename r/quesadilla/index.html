Cheese Quesadilla

100% vegetarian15 min · Easy

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pyaaz, shimla mirch aur corn jeere aur namak ke saath naram karo. 4 min

  2. 2

    Garam tawe pe tortilla rakho. Aadhe hisse pe cheese aur sabzi daalo.

  3. 3

    Mod kar dabao.

  4. 4

    Dono taraf sunehra senko. Tikone kaato. 4 min

Desi jugaad

Pighalne wala koi bhi cheese chalega, processed bhi.
