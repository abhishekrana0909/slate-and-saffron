South America · Paraguay

Sopa Paraguaya, Vegetarian Version

Makke, cheese aur pyaaz ki naram, thosi hui bake wali roti.

Time60 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pyaaz ko makkhan mein halka naram karo.

  2. 2

    Makke ka aata, bp aur namak milao, phir dahi, doodh, paneer aur corn daalo.

  3. 3

    Mixture ko greased tin mein daalo.

  4. 4

    180°C par oven mein 40 minute sunehra hone tak bake karo. 40 min

  5. 5

    Chouras tukdon mein katkar parso.

Desi Swap

Pyaaz ki zyada matra rakho, wahi iska asli swaad hai.

Chef’s Notes

Naam sopa hone par bhi ye ek thosi hui bake ki gayi roti hai. Aksar isme ande hote hain, yahan dahi aur doodh unki jagah lete hain.
