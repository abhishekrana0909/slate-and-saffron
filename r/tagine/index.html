Vegetable Tagine

Garam masalon wali dheemi aanch ki Morocco ki sabziyan.

100% vegetarian60 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chane bhigo kar ubaalo ya canned wale le lo. 20 min

  2. 2

    Tel mein pyaaz naram karo. Lehsun, adrak aur masale daalo. 6 min

  3. 3

    Tamatar daal kar thoda pakao. 4 min

  4. 4

    Sabzi aur chane ki parat lagao. Stock aur namak daalo.

  5. 5

    Dhak kar halki aanch pe naram hone tak pakao. Aakhir mein olives daalo. 40 min

Desi jugaad

Tagine na ho toh kase dhakkan wali bhaari kadhai le lo.

Story of this dish

Shanku jaise dhakkan wale mitti ke bartan par naam, jo bhaap ko wapas khaane mein girata hai.
