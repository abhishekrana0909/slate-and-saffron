Smashed Avocado Toast

Masali hui avocado, nimbu aur mirch ke saath khasta toast.

Time10 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Bread ko tawe ya toaster par khasta sek lo.

  2. 2

    Avocado ko kaante se masal kar nimbu, namak aur mirch milao.

  3. 3

    Toast par avocado phailao.

  4. 4

    Upar cherry tamatar ke tukde aur olive oil daalo.

Desi Swap

Paka hua avocado hi lena, kachcha swaad nahi dega.

Chef’s Notes

Australia ke cafe culture ka sabse mashhoor naashta, jise wahan ke brunch ki pehchaan mana jata hai.
