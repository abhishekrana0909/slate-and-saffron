Dal Dhokli

Meethi-khatti dal mein pakki masaledar atte ki patti.

Time50 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianGujaratiNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Atte mein haldi, mirch, namak aur thoda tel milakar sakht aata goondho aur patti jaisi chauras tukdon mein kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Arhar dal ko cooker mein naram ubaalo aur phento. 15 min

  3. 3

    Dal mein tamatar, imli, gud, moongfali aur masale daal kar ubaalo. 10 min

  4. 4

    Dhokli ke tukde ek ek karke daalo aur dheemi aanch pe 10 minute pakao. 10 min

  5. 5

    Ghee mein rai aur curry patta tadkao, dal mein daal kar garam parso.

Desi Swap

Dhokli daalne ke baad zyada mat hilao, ye toot sakti hain.

Chef’s Notes

Gujarat ka ek-bartan wala poora khaana, jo mausam badalte hi bahut yaad aata hai.
