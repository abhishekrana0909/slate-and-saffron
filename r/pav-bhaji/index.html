Mumbai Pav Bhaji

Makkhan wali masli hui sabzi aur sika hua pav.

100% vegetarian40 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo, gobhi aur matar bahut naram hone tak ubaal kar achhe se masal lo. 15 min

  2. 2

    Makkhan pighlao. Pyaaz aur lehsun bhuno, phir shimla mirch aur tamatar daalo. 8 min

  3. 3

    Masala, mirch aur namak daal kar galne tak pakao. 5 min

  4. 4

    Masli sabzi aur thoda paani daalo. Saath mein masal kar pakao. 10 min

  5. 5

    Pav makkhan mein senko. Pyaaz, nimbu aur dhaniye ke saath serve karo. 2 min

Desi jugaad

Pav bhaji masala na ho toh garam masala aur chutki bhar amchur le lo.

Story of this dish

Mumbai ki sadkon ki pasand, jo sheher ke kaamgaron ke jaldi ke khaane se judi maani jaati hai.
