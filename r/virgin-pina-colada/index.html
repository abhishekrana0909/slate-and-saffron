Virgin Pina Colada

Ananas aur nariyal ka malaidar blend.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Ananas kaat lo aur time ho toh freeze kar lo.

  2. 2

    Ananas, nariyal ka doodh, cheeni, nimbu ras aur barf peeso.

    Glass mein daal kar upar ananas ka tukda lagao.

Canned ananas bhi chalega. Syrup wala ho toh cheeni mat daalo.
