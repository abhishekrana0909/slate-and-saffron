Sopa de Quinoa

Andes ka poshak quinoa aur sabziyon ka halka soup.

Time40 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Quinoa ko achhe se dho lo.

  2. 2

    Tel mein pyaaz, lehsun aur jeera naram karo. 5 min

  3. 3

    Aloo, gajar, stock aur quinoa daal kar 20 minute pakao. 20 min

  4. 4

    Matar milao aur 5 minute aur pakao.

  5. 5

    Dhaniya chhidak kar garam parso.

Desi Swap

Quinoa ko dho kar hi daalo, nahi toh thoda kadwa lagta hai.

Chef’s Notes

Andes mein quinoa hazaaron saal se ugaya ja raha hai. Ye soup wahan thand mein garam rakhta hai.
