Rooibos Iced Tea

Laal jhaadi ki chai, shahad aur nimbu ke saath, barf pe.

Time15 min
LevelEasy
Alcohol0%
Serves4

0% alcoholGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Rooibos pe garam paani daal kar 8 minute bhigo, phir chhaan lo.

  2. 2

    Chai garam ho tabhi shahad ghol do.

  3. 3

    Thanda karke nimbu ras aur pudina daalo.

  4. 4

    Khoob saari barf ke upar serve karo.

Desi Swap

Rooibos na mile to saadi kaali chai le sakte ho.

Chef’s Notes

Rooibos sirf South Africa ke Western Cape mein ugti hai aur usme caffeine nahi hota.
