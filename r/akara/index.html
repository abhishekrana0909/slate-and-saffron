100% vegetarian35 min · Medium

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Lobia bhigo do. Chhilke ragad kar dho lo. 120 min

  2. 2

    Pyaaz, mirch aur bahut kam paani ke saath gaadha peeso.

  3. 3

    Hawa bharne ke liye zor se phento. Adrak aur namak daalo. 5 min

  4. 4

    Garam tel mein chammach se daalo. Charon taraf sunehra talo. 5 min

Desi jugaad

Bahut gaadha ghol akara ko tel peene se bachata hai.

Story of this dish

West Africa ka pasandida naashta, jo Brazil mein acaraje bankar pahuncha.
