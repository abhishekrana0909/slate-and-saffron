Atkilt Wat, Ethiopian Cabbage-Potato-Carrot Stew

Patta gobhi, aloo aur gajar ki haldi wali halki sabzi.

Time40 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Saari sabziyan bade tukdon mein kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Tel mein pyaaz bhuno, lehsun, adrak aur haldi daalo. 5 min

  3. 3

    Aloo aur gajar daal kar thoda paani milao aur dhak kar 10 minute pakao. 10 min

  4. 4

    Patta gobhi daal kar naram hone tak aur 10 minute pakao. 10 min

  5. 5

    Injera ya chapati ke saath garam parso.

Desi Swap

Haldi aur tel ka rang sabzi ko sunehra bana deta hai.

Chef’s Notes

Ethiopia ke vrat ke dino ki khaas sabzi, jo injera ke saath khayi jati hai.
