Cheese and Herb Scones

Naram, parat wali scones, cheese aur herbs ke saath.

Time35 min
LevelEasy
SpiceMild
Serves4

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, bp aur namak mila kar makkhan ko ungliyon se ragdo.

  2. 2

    Cheese aur parsley milao.

  3. 3

    Doodh daal kar halke haath se aata goondho.

  4. 4

    Mota bel kar gol kaat lo.

  5. 5

    200°C par oven mein 15 minute sunehra hone tak bake karo. 15 min

Desi Swap

Aata zyada goondhoge toh scones kadak ho jayenge.

Chef’s Notes

Scones Australia aur New Zealand ke chai-time ka roz ka hissa hain. Cheese ke saath ye namkeen roop sabse pasandida hai.
