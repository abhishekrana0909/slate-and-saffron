Vegetable Tamales

Makke ke aate ki naram parat, sabziyon ke saath bhaap mein pakki.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeVegetarian versionNo onion, no garlic90 min · HardSpice: Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Sookhe chhilke garam paani mein 30 minute bhigo do.

  2. 2

    Masa aata, makkhan, stock aur namak ko mila kar naram aata banao. 10 min

  3. 3

    Sabziyon, mirch aur cheese ko halka bhuno.

  4. 4

    Har chhilke par masa phailao, beech mein bharwan rakho aur lapet kar baandh do.

  5. 5

    Bhaap mein 45 minute pakao jab tak aata chhilke se alag na ho jaye. 45 min

Desi Swap

Chhilke na milen toh kele ke patte ya foil use karo.

Chef’s Notes

Tamales Mexico ke tyoharon ka khaana hai, jisme aksar maans hota hai. Yahan sirf sabziyan hain.
