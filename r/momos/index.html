Veg Momos

Raseeli sabziyon se bhare Himalaya ke steam momos.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version50 min · Hard

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Maida, namak aur paani se sakht aata goondh kar rakho. 20 min

  2. 2

    Sabziyan bahut bareek kaat kar paani nichod lo.

  3. 3

    Lehsun, adrak aur sabziyan jaldi bhuno. Soy sauce, kali mirch, namak daal kar thanda karo. 4 min

  4. 4

    Chhoti patli puriyan belo, bharo aur chunnat daal kar band karo.

  5. 5

    Tel lagi tray mein steam karo. 12 min

Desi jugaad

Steamer na ho toh ubalte paani pe channi rakh kar dhak do.

Story of this dish

Tibet aur Nepal ke pakwaan, jinme ghar pe aksar meat bhara jaata hai. Ab poore India ki sadkon ki pasand.
