Guacamole

Nimbu aur dhaniye wali malaidar avocado dip.

100% vegetarian10 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Avocado kaat kar gooda katori mein nikaalo.

  2. 2

    Nimbu ras aur namak daalo. Thode tukde chhod kar maslo.

  3. 3

    Kata pyaaz, tamatar, mirch aur dhaniya milao.

  4. 4

    Chips ke saath turant serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Nimbu ras avocado ko kaala hone se rokta hai.

Story of this dish

Iska naam Aztec shabd ahuacamolli se aaya hai, yaani avocado ki sauce.
