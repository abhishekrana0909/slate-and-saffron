Aloo Posto

Khus khus ke masale mein bane naram Bengali aloo.

100% vegetarianBengaliVeganGluten-free30 min · Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Khus khus ko 15 minute bhigo kar hari mirch ke saath bareek peeskar paste banao.

  2. 2

    Sarson ke tel ko garam karke pyaaz daalo aur halka bhoono. 5 min

  3. 3

    Aloo ke tukde, namak aur haldi daal kar 5 minute bhuno.

  4. 4

    Khus khus ka paste aur thoda paani daal kar dhak do aur aloo naram hone tak pakao. 15 min

  5. 5

    Upar se kaccha sarson ka tel chhidak kar garam chawal ke saath parso.

Desi jugaad

Kaccha sarson ka tel aakhir mein daalne se asli Bengali swaad aata hai.

Story of this dish

Bengal ki ghareloo rasoi ka mashhoor pakwan, jo garam chawal aur dal ke saath khaya jata hai.
