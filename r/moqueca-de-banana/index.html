Moqueca de Banana da Terra

Kachche kele aur nariyal ke doodh ka khushbudar curry.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free40 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Kele ko mote tukdon mein kaat kar nimbu aur namak lagao.

  2. 2

    Tel mein pyaaz, lehsun, tamatar aur shimla mirch naram karo. 8 min

  3. 3

    Nariyal ka doodh aur kele daalo.

  4. 4

    Dheemi aanch par 15 minute pakao jab tak kela naram ho jaye. 15 min

  5. 5

    Dhaniya chhidak kar chawal ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Kele ko ekdam gale mat do, tukde saabut rehne chahiye.

Chef’s Notes

Moqueca Brazil ka mashhoor stew hai jo aksar machhli se banta hai. Ye veg roop kachche kele aur sabziyon se banta hai.
