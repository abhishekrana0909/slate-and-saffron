Horchata

Chawal aur dalchini ka thanda malaidar doodh.

Time25 min
LevelEasy
Alcohol0%
Serves4

0% alcoholGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chawal aur dalchini ki chhadi ko paani mein 4 ghante ya raat bhar bhigo do.

  2. 2

    Chikna peeso aur mulayam kapde se chhaan lo.

  3. 3

    Doodh, cheeni aur vanilla milao.

  4. 4

    Thanda karke barf pe dalchini chhidak kar serve karo.

Desi Swap

Badam ya oat ka doodh lo to dairy-free ban jayega.

Chef’s Notes

Horchata Mexico ki sadkon aur taqueria ka drink hai jo bhigoye chawal, dalchini aur cheeni se banta hai.
