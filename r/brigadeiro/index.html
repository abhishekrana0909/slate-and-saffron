Brigadeiro

Brazil ke gaadhe chocolate truffles.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic25 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Condensed milk, cocoa aur makkhan dheemi aanch pe lagataar chalate hue pakao. 12 min

  2. 2

    Jab mixture bartan chhodne lage tab roko.

  3. 3

    Makkhan lagi plate pe daal kar poora thanda karo. 30 min

  4. 4

    Makkhan lage haathon se goliyan banao. Chocolate vermicelli mein lapeto.

Desi jugaad

Vermicelli na ho toh cocoa ya nariyal mein lapeto.

Story of this dish

Mana jaata hai ki naam 1940 ke dashak ke Brigadier Eduardo Gomes par pada. Brazil ke birthdays mein zaroori.
