Agua de Jamaica

Mexico ka thanda laal gudhal drink, dalchini ke halke swaad ke saath.

0% alcoholNo onion, no garlic20 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Gudhal aur dalchini aadhe paani mein ubaalo. 5 min

  2. 2

    Aanch se utaar kar bhigne do. 10 min

  3. 3

    Chaan lo, cheeni aur baaki paani milao.

  4. 4

    Thanda karke barf pe serve karo.

Desi jugaad

Thoda nimbu nichodne se rang aur swaad khil jata hai.

Story of this dish

Mexico ke aguas frescas mein se ek, yaani phal aur phoolon ke halke drinks jo har jagah milte hain.
