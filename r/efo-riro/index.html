Efo Riro, Spinach Stew Vegetarian Version

Palak aur lal mirch ki gaadhi, teekhi Nigerian sabzi.

Time40 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-freeVegetarian version

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Tamatar, shimla mirch, pyaaz aur mirch ko peeskar puree banao.

  2. 2

    Tel garam karke puree ko 15 minute pakao jab tak tel upar na aaye. 15 min

  3. 3

    Mushroom ya soya daal kar milao.

  4. 4

    Bareek kata palak daalkar 5 minute pakao. 5 min

  5. 5

    Chawal ya yam ke saath garam parso.

Desi Swap

Puree ko achhe se pakao, kachchi tamatar ka swaad aachha nahi lagta.

Chef’s Notes

Nigeria ka mashhoor palak ka stew, jisme aksar machhli ya maans hota hai. Yahan sirf sabziyan aur mirch ki gehraai hai.
