Mushroom Pie

Gaadhi mushroom gravy bhari garam pie.

100% vegetarian60 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Makkhan mein pyaaz, lehsun aur mushroom bhuno. 8 min

  2. 2

    Maida milao aur stock daal kar gaadha banao.

  3. 3

    Thyme milao aur 8 minute pakao, phir thanda karo. 8 min

  4. 4

    Pie dish mein pastry bichhao, bharwan daalo aur upar se pastry dhako.

  5. 5

    Doodh lagakar 200°C par 25 minute sunehra hone tak bake karo. 25 min

Desi Swap

Bharwan thanda karke hi daalo, warna pastry geeli ho jayegi.

Chef’s Notes

Meat pie Australia ka rashtriya snack hai. Ye veg roop usi ke andaaz mein mushroom ki gravy se banta hai.
