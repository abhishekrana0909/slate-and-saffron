Patatas Bravas

Dhuandaar teekhi tamatar sauce mein karare aloo.

Time40 min
LevelEasy
SpiceSpicy
Serves4

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Aloo ke tukde karke lagbhag naram hone tak ubaalo aur chaan lo. 8 min

  2. 2

    Aloo bahut karare hone tak talo ya bhuno. 20 min

  3. 3

    Tel mein lehsun garam karo. Paprika, mirch, puree, cheeni aur sirka daal kar pakao. 8 min

  4. 4

    Aloo pe namak daalo aur upar sauce daalo.

Desi Swap

Paprika na ho toh Kashmiri mirch wahi rang degi.

Chef’s Notes

Spain ka classic tapas. Bravas ka matlab hai tez, sauce ke teekhepan ke liye.
