Southern Cornbread, Vegetarian Version

Sunehri, thodi meethi makke ki roti jaisi cake, bina ande ke.

100% vegetarianVegetarian versionNo onion, no garlic40 min · EasySpice: Mild

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Dono aate, baking powder, cheeni aur namak milao.

  2. 2

    Dahi, doodh aur pighla makkhan mila kar halka sa mix karo. 3 min

  3. 3

    Garam ghee lage kadhai ya tin mein batter daalo.

  4. 4

    200°C par oven mein 25 minute sunehra hone tak bake karo. 25 min

  5. 5

    Garam garam makkhan ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Oven na ho toh bhaari kadhai mein dhak kar dheemi aanch pe pakao.

Chef’s Notes

Southern America ki rasoi ka khaas hissa, jo aksar ande aur chhaachh ke saath banta hai. Yahan dahi aur makkhan hi kaafi hain.
