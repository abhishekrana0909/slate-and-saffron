Elote, Mexican Street Corn

Aanch pe bhuna bhutta, cheese, nimbu aur mirch ke saath.

100% vegetarianGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic20 min · EasySpice: Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Bhutte ko seedhe aanch par har taraf se halka kaala hone tak bhuno.

  2. 2

    Garam bhutte par malai ya mayonnaise ki parat lagao. 2 min

  3. 3

    Upar se paneer ka chura, mirch aur namak chhidko.

  4. 4

    Nimbu nichod kar aur dhaniya daal kar turant khao.

Desi Swap

Paneer ki jagah crumbled feta ya cheese bhi chalega.

Chef’s Notes

Mexico ki sadkon par thele ka sabse mashhoor naashta, jo bhune bhutte par malai aur cheese lagakar banta hai.
