Kadai Paneer

Shimla mirch aur paneer, taaze pise masale ke saath.

100% vegetarianNorth IndianGluten-free30 min · MediumSpice: Medium

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Sabut dhaniya aur sukhi laal mirch ko halka bhuno aur dardara kooto.

  2. 2

    Tel garam karke pyaaz, adrak aur lehsun bhuno, phir tamatar aur namak daal kar galao. 10 min

  3. 3

    Kuta hua masala aur garam masala daal kar tel alag hone tak pakao.

  4. 4

    Shimla mirch ke bade tukde aur paneer daalo. 5 minute tez aanch pe halka sa pakao taaki sabzi karari rahe. 5 min

  5. 5

    Kasuri methi masal kar daalo aur garam serve karo.

Desi Swap

Shimla mirch ko aakhir mein daalna, tabhi uska karara swaad bachta hai.

Chef’s Notes

Kadai yaani lohe ki kadhai mein banne wali sabzi, jisme sabut masale kootkar daale jaate hain.
