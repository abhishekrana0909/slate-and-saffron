Taameya, Egyptian Falafel

Hare masale wale fava beans ke karare Egyptian falafel.

100% vegetarianVeganGluten-free40 min · MediumSpice: Mild

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Beans bhigo kar chhilke utaar lo.

  2. 2

    Hare patte, pyaaz, lehsun aur masalon ke saath bareek peeso.

  3. 3

    Til milao aur chhoti tikkiyan banakar 15 minute rakho. 15 min

  4. 4

    Garam tel mein sunehra karari hone tak talo. 8 min

  5. 5

    Pita, tahini aur salad ke saath parso.

Desi Swap

Til upar se lagakar talne se tikkiyan aur karari banti hain.

Chef’s Notes

Falafel ka Egyptian roop. Chane ki jagah yahan fava beans se bani hari tikkiyan hoti hain.
