Anzac Biscuits

Oats aur nariyal ke chewy biscuits, bina ande ke.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic30 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Oven 170°C pe garam karo. Oats, maida, nariyal aur cheeni milao. 10 min

  2. 2

    Makkhan aur syrup pighlao. Garam paani mein soda mila kar daalo, jhaag aayega.

  3. 3

    Geela aur sukha mila do.

  4. 4

    Goliyan bana kar tray pe chapta karo aur sunehra bake karo. 14 min

Desi jugaad

Golden syrup na ho toh shahad ya gud ki chashni le lo.
