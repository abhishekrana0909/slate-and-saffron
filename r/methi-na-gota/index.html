Methi na Gota

Methi aur besan ke kurkure, garam pakode.

Time30 min
LevelMedium
SpiceMedium
Serves4

100% vegetarianGujaratiVeganGluten-freeNo onion, no garlic

What you need

How to make it

  1. 1

    Methi bareek kaat lo.

  2. 2

    Besan, methi, kootha dhaniya, hari mirch, cheeni aur namak milao.

  3. 3

    Thoda paani daal kar gaadha ghol banao.

  4. 4

    Garam tel mein chhote golay daal kar sunehra talo. 10 min

  5. 5

    Kadhi ya chutney ke saath garam parso.

Desi Swap

Cheeni ki ek chutki gota ko sunehra rang aur halka meetha swaad deti hai.

Chef’s Notes

Barsaat mein Gujarat ke har ghar mein methi na gota chai ke saath banate hain.
