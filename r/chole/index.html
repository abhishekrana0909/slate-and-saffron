Punjabi Chole

Amritsar ke dhabon jaise gehre rang ke chatpate chole.

100% vegetarianNorth IndianVeganGluten-free50 min · MediumSpice: Spicy

Your rating
WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Chane raat bhar bhigo do. Namak aur taaze paani ke saath cooker mein naram hone tak pakao. 20 min

  2. 2

    Tel garam karo. Jeera aur tej patta daalo, phir kata pyaaz gehra bhoora hone tak bhuno. 10 min

  3. 3

    Adrak, lehsun aur hari mirch daal kar ek minute chalao.

  4. 4

    Tamatar aur saare masale daalo. Tel alag hone tak pakao. 8 min

  5. 5

    Chane thode paani ke saath daalo. Kuch chane masal kar dheemi aanch pe pakao. 15 min

  6. 6

    Hara dhaniya daal kar garam serve karo.

Desi Swap

Gehre rang ke liye chane ubaalte waqt ek tea bag daal do.

Chef’s Notes

Punjab ki roz ki daawat, aksar kulche ya bhature ke saath.
