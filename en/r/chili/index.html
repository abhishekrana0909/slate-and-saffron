Three Bean Chili

Smoky Texas-style chili made fully with beans.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version40 min · Easy

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak beans overnight and pressure cook until soft. 25 min

  2. 2

    Fry onion, capsicum and garlic. 6 min

  3. 3

    Add spices, then tomato. Cook down. 8 min

  4. 4

    Add beans, corn, salt and some cooking water. Simmer until thick. 20 min

  5. 5

    Top with curd and serve.

Desi jugaad

No black beans? Use more rajma.

Story of this dish

Chili is the official dish of Texas, normally made with beef. Ours is all beans.
