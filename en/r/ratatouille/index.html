A colourful French bake of summer vegetables.

100% vegetarian60 min · Medium

How to make it

  1. 1

    Fry chopped onion, garlic and capsicum. Add 2 chopped tomatoes and cook into a sauce. 10 min

  2. 2

    Spread sauce in a baking dish.

  3. 3

    Slice eggplant, zucchini and remaining tomatoes thinly.

  4. 4

    Arrange slices in a spiral. Brush with oil, oregano and salt.

  5. 5

    Cover and bake at 180°C, then uncover until golden. 45 min
