Guacamole

Creamy avocado dip with lime and coriander.

100% vegetarian10 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Halve avocados and scoop into a bowl.

  2. 2

    Add lemon juice and salt. Mash, leaving some chunks.

  3. 3

    Fold in chopped onion, tomato, chilli and coriander.

  4. 4

    Serve right away with chips.

Desi jugaad

Lemon juice stops the avocado from going brown.

Story of this dish

Its name comes from the Aztec word ahuacamolli, meaning avocado sauce.
