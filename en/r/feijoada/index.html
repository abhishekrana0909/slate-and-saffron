Veg Feijoada

A dark, deep Brazilian black bean stew.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version60 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak beans overnight. Pressure cook with bay leaves until soft. 30 min

  2. 2

    Fry onion and garlic. Add carrot, capsicum and tomato. 8 min

  3. 3

    Add paprika, cumin and salt.

  4. 4

    Add beans and simmer, mashing some, until thick and dark. 25 min

  5. 5

    Serve over boiled rice with orange slices if you like.

Story of this dish

Brazil's famous stew is traditionally packed with pork. Ours keeps the beans and smoky depth.
