Vegetable Pad Thai

Sweet, sour, nutty rice noodles from Thailand.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version25 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak noodles in hot water until just bendable. Drain. 8 min

  2. 2

    Mix tamarind, soy, sugar and chilli for the sauce.

  3. 3

    Fry tofu cubes until golden. Add garlic and carrot. 5 min

  4. 4

    Add noodles and sauce. Toss on high heat. 3 min

  5. 5

    Add sprouts and spring onion. Top with peanuts and lemon.

Desi jugaad

No tofu? Lightly fried paneer works well.

No rice noodles? Use thin flat noodles.

Story of this dish

Thailand's government promoted pad thai as a national dish around the 1930s and 40s. Ours skips the usual fish sauce and egg.
