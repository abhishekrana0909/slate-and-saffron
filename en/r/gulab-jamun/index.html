Gulab Jamun

Soft milk dumplings soaked in rose syrup.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic45 min · Hard

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Boil sugar, water and cardamom for 5 minutes. Add rose water and keep warm. 5 min

  2. 2

    Knead khoya, flour and soda gently, adding milk until smooth.

  3. 3

    Roll into small balls with no cracks.

  4. 4

    Fry in ghee on low heat, moving gently, until deep brown. 10 min

  5. 5

    Soak in warm syrup before serving. 60 min

Desi jugaad

No khoya? Use milk powder with a little cream and ghee.

Story of this dish

Its name joins the Persian word for rose water with the jamun fruit it resembles.
