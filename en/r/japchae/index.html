Japchae

Glossy Korean glass noodles with colourful vegetables.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version30 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

What you need

How to make it

  1. 1

    Boil noodles until soft, rinse, and cut with scissors. 6 min

  2. 2

    Mix soy, sugar, sesame oil and garlic.

  3. 3

    Blanch spinach and squeeze it dry. 1 min

  4. 4

    Stir-fry onion, carrot, capsicum and mushroom one by one. 6 min

  5. 5

    Toss noodles, vegetables and sauce together. Sprinkle sesame. 3 min

Desi jugaad

No glass noodles? Use thin rice vermicelli.

Story of this dish

A festive Korean dish, often made with beef. This one keeps only the vegetables.
