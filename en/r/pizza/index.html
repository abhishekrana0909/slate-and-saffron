Pizza Margherita

Tomato, mozzarella and basil on a crisp base.

100% vegetarian40 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Mix warm water, yeast and sugar. Add flour, salt and oil. Knead smooth and let it rise. 60 min

  2. 2

    Cook tomato puree, garlic and salt for a few minutes. 5 min

  3. 3

    Heat the oven to its highest setting with a tray inside. 15 min

  4. 4

    Stretch the dough thin. Add sauce and torn mozzarella.

  5. 5

    Bake until blistered. Add basil and olive oil. 9 min

Desi jugaad

No oven? Cook covered in a heavy pan on low heat.

Story of this dish

Popular legend links this pizza to Naples and Queen Margherita in 1889, with the colours of Italy's flag.
