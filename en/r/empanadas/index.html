Cheese Corn Empanadas

Hand-crimped Argentine pastries with a cheesy corn filling.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version60 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Rub butter into flour and salt. Add water, knead and chill. 30 min

  2. 2

    Soften onion, add corn and paprika, and cool. Mix in cheese. 6 min

  3. 3

    Roll dough and cut large circles. Fill, fold and crimp the edges.

  4. 4

    Brush with milk. Bake at 200°C until golden. 25 min

Desi jugaad

No oven? Deep fry on medium heat.

Story of this dish

From the Spanish word empanar, to wrap in bread. Beef is common, but cheese and corn is a classic too.
