Koshari

Rice, lentils and pasta under spicy tomato and crispy onions.

100% vegetarian50 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak chickpeas overnight and boil until soft. Or use canned. 20 min

  2. 2

    Fry thin onion slices until crisp. Keep the oil. 12 min

  3. 3

    Cook lentils and rice together in onion oil and water until done. 20 min

  4. 4

    Boil macaroni separately. 9 min

  5. 5

    Simmer puree, garlic, cumin, chilli, vinegar and salt. 10 min

  6. 6

    Layer everything, top with sauce and crisp onions.

Desi jugaad

Red masoor is easy to find. Brown lentils are more traditional.
