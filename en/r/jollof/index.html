Jollof Rice

West Africa's smoky one-pot tomato rice.

100% vegetarianVegetarian version50 min · Medium

This dish is often made with meat or egg elsewhere. This is our vegetarian version.

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Blend tomato, capsicum, one onion, ginger and garlic.

  2. 2

    Fry the other onion. Add puree and spices, and fry for a few minutes. 4 min

  3. 3

    Add blended mix and cook until thick. 15 min

  4. 4

    Add washed rice, stock, bay and salt. Cover tightly and cook on low. 25 min

  5. 5

    Let the bottom catch slightly for smokiness. Fluff and serve.

Desi jugaad

No stock? Use water with a little extra spice.

Story of this dish

Named after the Wolof people of Senegambia. Often cooked with meat stock, ours uses vegetable stock.
