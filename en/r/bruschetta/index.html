Tomato Bruschetta

Garlic-rubbed toast piled with juicy tomatoes.

100% vegetarian15 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Dice tomatoes. Mix with torn basil, oil, salt and pepper. 5 min

  2. 2

    Toast bread slices until crisp. 4 min

  3. 3

    Rub each slice with a cut garlic clove.

  4. 4

    Spoon tomatoes on top and serve right away.

Desi jugaad

No baguette? Toast thick slices of any bread.

Story of this dish

Italian farmers once toasted bread to taste newly pressed olive oil.
