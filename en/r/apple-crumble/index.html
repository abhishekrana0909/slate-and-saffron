Apple Crumble

Warm spiced apples under a buttery golden crumb.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic50 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Slice apples. Toss with half the sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice. Put in a baking dish.

  2. 2

    Rub cold butter into flour until crumbly.

  3. 3

    Stir in the remaining sugar and oats.

  4. 4

    Scatter over the apples. Bake at 180°C until golden. 35 min

  5. 5

    Serve warm with cream.

Desi jugaad

No oven? Cook apples in a pan and toast the crumb separately.

Story of this dish

A British favourite widely linked to Second World War rationing, when pastry ingredients were short.
