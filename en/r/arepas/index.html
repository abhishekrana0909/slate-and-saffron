Cheese Arepas

Crisp corn cakes stuffed with melting cheese.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic30 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Mix corn flour, salt and warm water. Rest until firm. 5 min

  2. 2

    Shape thick discs about the size of your palm.

  3. 3

    Cook on an oiled tawa on both sides until a crust forms. 12 min

  4. 4

    Split open with a knife and fill with cheese and butter.

Desi jugaad

Regular makki atta is coarser. Knead it with hot water and handle gently.

Story of this dish

Corn cakes eaten in Venezuela and Colombia since before Europeans arrived.
