Paneer Butter Masala

Soft paneer in a silky tomato and butter gravy.

100% vegetarian35 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Simmer tomato, onion, cashew, ginger and garlic with a little water until soft. 12 min

  2. 2

    Cool, blend smooth and strain.

  3. 3

    Melt butter. Add chilli powder, then the puree. Cook until it thickens. 10 min

  4. 4

    Add salt, sugar, garam masala and crushed kasuri methi.

  5. 5

    Add paneer cubes and cream. Simmer gently, then serve. 4 min

Desi jugaad

No cream? Blend 4 extra cashews with milk.

Story of this dish

The creamy tomato gravy became famous through Delhi's Punjabi restaurants after 1947.
