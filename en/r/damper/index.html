Damper Bread

Simple Australian soda bread with a golden crust.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic45 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Heat oven to 200°C. Mix flour, baking powder and salt. 10 min

  2. 2

    Rub in butter. Add milk and mix into a soft dough.

  3. 3

    Shape a round and cut a cross on top.

  4. 4

    Bake until it sounds hollow when tapped. 30 min

Desi jugaad

No oven? Bake in a heavy covered pot on low heat.

Story of this dish

Traditionally baked in campfire coals by Australian stockmen and travellers.
