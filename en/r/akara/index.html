Akara

Airy West African black-eyed pea fritters.

100% vegetarian35 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak black-eyed peas. Rub off the skins and rinse. 120 min

  2. 2

    Blend with onion, chilli and very little water into a thick batter.

  3. 3

    Whisk the batter hard to add air. Add ginger and salt. 5 min

  4. 4

    Drop spoonfuls into hot oil. Fry until golden on all sides. 5 min

Desi jugaad

A very thick batter keeps akara from soaking oil.

Story of this dish

A beloved breakfast in West Africa that also travelled to Brazil as acarajé.
