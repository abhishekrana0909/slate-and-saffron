Mac and Cheese

Macaroni in a rich, velvety cheese sauce.

100% vegetarianNo onion, no garlic25 min · Easy

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Boil macaroni in salted water. 9 min

  2. 2

    Melt butter, stir in flour and cook briefly. 2 min

  3. 3

    Whisk in milk slowly and cook until thick. 6 min

  4. 4

    Off the heat, add cheese, mustard, pepper and salt.

  5. 5

    Fold in macaroni and serve hot.
