Vegetable Spring Rolls

Crisp golden rolls full of crunchy vegetables.

100% vegetarian45 min · Medium

WhatsApp

What you need

People4

How to make it

  1. 1

    Mix flour, salt and water into a soft dough. Rest, then roll very thin squares and cook lightly on a tawa. 20 min

  2. 2

    Stir-fry garlic and vegetables on high heat. Add soy, pepper and salt. Cool. 4 min

  3. 3

    Place filling on a sheet, fold the sides, and roll tight. Seal with flour paste.

  4. 4

    Fry on medium heat until golden. 5 min

Desi jugaad

Short on time? Buy ready spring roll sheets.

Story of this dish

Named for China's Spring Festival, when these golden rolls are served as a wish for plenty.
