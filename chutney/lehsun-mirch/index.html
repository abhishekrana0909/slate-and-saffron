Rajasthani chutney

Lehsun-Mirch

Rajasthan ki pehchaan: sookhi laal mirch aur lehsun ki aag jaisi chutney. Thodi si hi kaafi hoti hai.

15 minBanta hai 1/2 cup2 hafte fridge meinSpicy

Saamagri

  • 10-12 sookhi laal mirch (Mathania ya Kashmiri)
  • 10-12 lehsun ki kaliyan
  • 1 tsp jeera
  • 1/2 tsp namak
  • 1 tbsp tel
  • 1 tsp nimbu ka ras
  • 1/4 cup garam paani

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Sookhi mirch ke dande tod kar 15 minute garam paani mein bhigo do.
  2. Silbatte ya mixer mein lehsun, jeera aur namak daalo aur mota kootlo.
  3. Bhigi hui mirch daalo aur thoda bhiga hua paani le kar gaadha paste banao.
  4. Kadhai mein 1 tbsp tel garam karo aur paste ko 2 minute halka bhuno.
  5. Thanda hone par nimbu ka ras milao aur kaanch ke dibbe mein bharo.

Chef ke tips

  • Teekhapan kam karna ho to beej nikaal kar mirch bhigo.
  • Upar se thoda tel dalkar rakhne se chutney lambe samay tak chalti hai.
  • Seedhi roti par lagakar ghee ke saath khaao, bas ek chamach kaafi hai.
