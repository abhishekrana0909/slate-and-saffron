Bengali chutney

Tamatar-Khejur

Bengal ki shaadi wali khatti-meethi chutney: pake tamatar, khajoor aur panch phoron.

25 minBanta hai 1 cup1 hafta fridge meinMild

Saamagri

  • 3 pake tamatar, kate hue
  • 8-10 khajoor (guthli nikaali hui), kate hue
  • 1 tsp panch phoron
  • 1 tbsp sarson ka tel
  • 3 tbsp cheeni
  • 1/2 tsp namak
  • 1/2 cup paani
  • 1 sookhi laal mirch

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Pan mein sarson ka tel garam karo, panch phoron aur sookhi mirch ka tadka lagao.
  2. Tamatar daal kar 5 minute pakao jab tak naram ho jaaye.
  3. Kate hue khajoor, namak aur paani daalo, dhak kar 5 minute pakao.
  4. Cheeni daalo aur 8-10 minute aur pakao jab tak chutney gaadhi aur chamakdar ho jaaye.
  5. Thanda karke parosao, ye thandi bhi achhi lagti hai.

Chef ke tips

  • Sarson ka tel ka halka kasaila swaad hi asli Bengali chutney ki pehchaan hai.
  • Khajoor kam ya zyada karke meethapan badal sakte ho.
  • Papad ke saath ye khaas jodi hai.
