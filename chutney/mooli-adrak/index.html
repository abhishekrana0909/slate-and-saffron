Punjabi chutney

Mooli-Adrak

Sardiyon ki mooli aur taaze adrak ki teekhi chutney. Makki di roti aur sarson ke saag ka best saathi.

15 minBanta hai 1 cup2 din fridge meinMedium

Saamagri

  • 1 cup kasi hui safed mooli
  • 1 inch adrak, kasa hua
  • 2 hari mirch, kati hui
  • 1 nimbu ka ras
  • 1/2 tsp bhuna jeera powder
  • swaad anusaar namak
  • 1 tsp sarson ka tel (optional)

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Mooli ko chhilkar bareek kas lo.
  2. Kasi mooli ko haath se achhe se nichod kar paani alag kar do, warna chutney paani chhodegi.
  3. Ek bowl mein mooli, adrak, hari mirch, jeera powder aur namak milao.
  4. Nimbu ka ras aur chaho to kachche sarson ke tel ki 1 boond daalo.
  5. Chamche se achhe se mila kar 10 minute dhak kar rakh do, swaad ghul jayega.

Chef ke tips

  • Nichoda hua mooli ka paani sabzi ya dal mein daal sakte ho.
  • Sirf taazi aur kadak mooli lo, nami wali mooli kadwi hoti hai.
  • Banane ke 1-2 ghante mein hi parosao, jaldi paani chhodti hai.
