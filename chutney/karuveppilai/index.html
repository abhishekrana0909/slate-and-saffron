South Indian chutney

Karuveppilai

Curry patte (karuveppilai) ki sookhi-si chutney. Khushboo dar aur lohe-sa swasth.

20 minBanta hai 3/4 cup5 din fridge meinMedium

Saamagri

  • 1 cup curry patte (taaze)
  • 1 tbsp chana dal
  • 1 tbsp urad dal
  • 3-4 sookhi laal mirch
  • 1 tbsp imli ka gooda
  • 2 tbsp nariyal kas (optional)
  • 1 tbsp tel
  • swaad anusaar namak

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Curry patte dhokar poori tarah sukha lo, geele patte nahi chalenge.
  2. Pan mein tel garam karo, chana dal aur urad dal sunehri hone tak bhuno.
  3. Sookhi mirch daalo, phir curry patte daal kar 2-3 minute kadak hone tak bhuno.
  4. Thanda hone par imli, namak aur nariyal ke saath mixer mein peeso, zarurat ho to 1-2 tbsp paani lo.
  5. Mota, thoda daaredar peeso.

Chef ke tips

  • Patte jab kadak ho jaayein tabhi aanch band karo, jalna nahi chahiye.
  • Garam chawal par 1 chamach til ya nariyal ka tel dalkar khaao.
  • Sookhi hone ki wajah se doosre chutneyon se zyada din chalti hai.
