Street Food chutney

Meethi Imli

Chaat ki jaan: gehri, khatti-meethi imli ki chutney. Ek baar banao, hafton chalegi.

30 minBanta hai 1 cup2 hafte fridge meinMild

Saamagri

  • 100 g imli (beej nikaali hui)
  • 3/4 cup gud ya brown sugar
  • 2 cup paani
  • 1 tsp saunth (sukhi adrak) powder
  • 1 tsp bhuna jeera powder
  • 1/2 tsp laal mirch powder
  • 1/2 tsp kala namak
  • swaad anusaar namak

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Imli ko 2 cup garam paani mein 30 minute bhigo kar haath se masal lo.
  2. Chhalni se chhaan kar gaadha ras alag karo, rekshe aur beej hata do.
  3. Ras ko pan mein daalo aur gud daal kar dheemi aanch par ubalo.
  4. Saunth, jeera, laal mirch aur dono namak daalo, 10-12 minute pakao jab tak chutney gaadhi ho jaaye.
  5. Thanda hone par aur gaadhi ho jaati hai, zarurat ho to thoda paani milao.

Chef ke tips

  • Kadhai ki jagah steel ya non-stick pan lo, imli loha ya kaansa mein kaali ho jaati hai.
  • Chutney ko hamesha chamche se chala kar dekho, chamche se girne par patli dhaar honi chahiye.
  • Ice-cube tray mein jama lo, jitni chahiye utni nikaal lo.
