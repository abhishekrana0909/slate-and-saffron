Maharashtrian chutney

Thecha

Kolhapur-Pune ka thecha: hari mirch aur lehsun ko kootkar banaya hua jhanjhanata teekha mel.

10 minBanta hai 1/2 cup1 hafta fridge meinSpicy

Saamagri

  • 10-12 hari mirch (teekhi wali)
  • 8-10 lehsun ki kaliyan
  • 1/4 cup bhuni moongfali, mota kuta hua
  • 1 tbsp tel
  • swaad anusaar namak
  • 1 tsp nimbu ka ras

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Kadhai mein 1 tsp tel garam karo, hari mirch halki bhuno jab tak chhilke dhabbedar ho jaayein.
  2. Silbatte (ya khal-musal) mein lehsun, namak aur bhuni mirch daalo.
  3. Mota-mota kootlo, mixer se bilkul chikna mat peeso.
  4. Kuti moongfali milao, upar se garam tel daalo aur nimbu ras milao.

Chef ke tips

  • Mirch ke beej nikaal do to teekhapan kam ho jaata hai.
  • Bhakri ke saath 1 chhota chamach hi kaafi hai.
  • Silbatte mein kootne se asli swaad aata hai.
