South Indian chutney

Tamatar-Lehsun

Bhune tamatar aur lehsun ki laal chutney, thodi khatti aur thodi teekhi. Dosa ke saath behtareen.

20 minBanta hai 1 cup5 din fridge meinMedium

Saamagri

  • 3 pake tamatar, kate hue
  • 5-6 lehsun ki kaliyan
  • 3-4 sookhi laal mirch
  • 1 tbsp imli ka gooda
  • 2 tbsp tel
  • 1/2 tsp rai
  • 8 curry patte
  • swaad anusaar namak

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Pan mein 1 tbsp tel garam karo, sookhi mirch aur lehsun halka bhuno.
  2. Tamatar daalo, namak daal kar dhak do aur 8-10 minute pakao jab tak tamatar gal jaaye aur paani sookh jaaye.
  3. Imli daal kar 1 minute aur pakao, phir thanda hone do.
  4. Mixer mein thoda mota peeso.
  5. Alag pan mein baaki tel garam karo, rai aur curry patte ka tadka banao aur chutney par daalo.

Chef ke tips

  • Tamatar jitne pakke honge, chutney utni meethi aur laal banegi.
  • Tadke mein til ka tel (gingelly) daalne se South style swaad aata hai.
  • Fridge mein tel ke saath rakho, 5 din tak theek rahegi.
