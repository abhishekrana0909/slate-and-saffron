Maharashtrian chutney

Til ki Chutney

Bhune til aur sookhe nariyal ki sookhi chutney. Mitti ki khushboo wali, zyada din chalne wali.

15 minBanta hai 3/4 cup1 hafta fridge meinMild

Saamagri

  • 1/2 cup safed til
  • 1/4 cup sookha nariyal kas
  • 3-4 lehsun ki kaliyan
  • 1-2 tsp laal mirch powder
  • 1 tsp jeera
  • swaad anusaar namak
  • 1 tsp nimbu ka ras (optional)

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Til ko sukhi kadhai mein halka sunehra hone tak bhuno, jalne mat do.
  2. Sookhe nariyal ko bhi alag se halka bhuno.
  3. Thanda hone par til, nariyal, lehsun, jeera, mirch aur namak mixer mein daalo.
  4. Sookha, mota-mota peeso.
  5. Chaho to nimbu ka ras milao aur dibbe mein bharo.

Chef ke tips

  • Mixer ko zyada mat chalao, til ka tel nikalne lagta hai.
  • Dahi mila kar patli chutney bhi bana sakte ho.
  • Roti par ghee lagakar upar se chhidak kar khaao.
