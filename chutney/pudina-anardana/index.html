Punjabi chutney

Pudina-Anardana

Anardana (sukhe anaar ke daane) ki khatai wali chutney. Parathe aur kachori ke saath kamaal lagti hai.

15 minBanta hai 3/4 cup4 din fridge meinMedium

Saamagri

  • 1 cup pudina patte
  • 2 tbsp anardana
  • 1 tsp jeera
  • 2 hari mirch
  • 1 tbsp gud (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp kala namak
  • swaad anusaar namak
  • 1/4 cup garam paani

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Anardana ko garam paani mein 10 minute bhigo do.
  2. Jeera ko sukhi kadhai mein halka bhuno jab tak khushboo aaye.
  3. Bhigaya anardana (paani samet), jeera, pudina, hari mirch, gud aur dono namak mixer mein daalo.
  4. Thoda-thoda paani daalte hue darra-darra ya chikna, jaisa pasand ho, peeso.
  5. Kaanch ki saaf bottle mein bharo aur 30 minute baad parosao taaki swaad ghul jaye.

Chef ke tips

  • Anardana ko sukha bhunkar peesne se aur gehra swaad aata hai.
  • Gud kam ya zyada karke khatte-meethe ka santulan badal sakte ho.
  • Fridge mein rakhne par 1 din baad swaad aur achha ho jaata hai.
