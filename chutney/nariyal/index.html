South Indian chutney

Nariyal

Idli-dosa ki sabse pasandida saathi: taaze nariyal aur bhune chane ki halki chutney.

15 minBanta hai 1 cup1 din fridge meinMild

Saamagri

  • 1 cup taaza nariyal, kasa hua
  • 2 tbsp bhuna chana (dalia)
  • 1-2 hari mirch
  • 1/2 inch adrak
  • 1/2 cup paani ya chhaachh
  • swaad anusaar namak
  • 1 tsp tel (tadke ke liye)
  • 1/2 tsp rai
  • 8-10 curry patte
  • 1 sookhi laal mirch

Banane ka tareeka

  1. Nariyal, bhuna chana, hari mirch, adrak aur namak mixer mein daalo.
  2. Thoda-thoda paani daal kar mulayam, thoda gaadha peeso aur bowl mein nikaal lo.
  3. Chhote pan mein tel garam karo, rai daalo aur chatakne do.
  4. Curry patte aur tooti hui sookhi mirch daal kar 10 second bhuno.
  5. Garam tadka chutney par daalo aur halka sa mila lo.

Chef ke tips

  • Sirf taaza nariyal lo, sukhe nariyal mein swaad nahi aata.
  • Dahi ya chhaachh dalne se chutney thodi khatti aur halki ho jaati hai.
  • Isi din khaa lo, nariyal ki chutney jaldi kharab hoti hai.
